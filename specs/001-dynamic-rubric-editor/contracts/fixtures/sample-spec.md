# Senior Data Platform Engineer

Department: Data Engineering
Organization: Northwind Analytics

Northwind Analytics is hiring a Senior Data Platform Engineer to build reliable, compliant, and observable data products for internal and external teams.

## Responsibilities

- Design and operate Azure data pipelines, mentor junior engineers, and document production support procedures.
- Partner with recruiters and hiring managers to translate business requirements into maintainable scoring rubrics.
- Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Required Qualifications

- Must have 5+ years of backend or platform engineering experience and proven experience with distributed systems.
- Must have expert SQL, Python, and Azure Data Factory experience.
- Must hold an Azure Data Engineer Associate certification.
- Must have experience implementing CI/CD for data workloads.
- Must have excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Must have 5+ years of backend or platform engineering experience.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Nice to have experience with Spark, Databricks, or Synapse analytics.
- Preferred experience in regulated industries and creating audit-ready diagnostics.
- Bonus points for leading architecture reviews.

## Hiring Rubric

- Technical Platform Skills — 50%
- Delivery and Quality — 20%
- Collaboration and Communication — 20%
- Leadership and Growth — 10%
